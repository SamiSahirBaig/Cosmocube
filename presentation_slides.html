The Downlink Math Problem

A standard low-noise camera generates 186 Megabits of raw image data every second. Over an orbit, this generates 125 Gigabytes of video data.

A 1U CubeSat is limited to an amateur UHF radio link at only 9.6 kbps. Downloading a single full-resolution image takes over 30 minutes.
